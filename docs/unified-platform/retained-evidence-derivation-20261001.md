# Retained participation successor evidence

A frozen evaluation mistakenly included incumbents of approved reserved annual assignments that were explicitly closed for this bid. A successor must correct those participation facts without changing the historical freeze, earlier versions or their sessions.

The optional `evidenceFreeze.derivation` method is `VERIFIED_RESERVED_RETENTION`, version 1. Absence preserves the original normalized evaluation path. The method binds an independently loaded immutable baseline version ID/hash, original capture source-version ID/hash, freeze ID/evaluation digest, raw personnel/credential digests, evaluator revision, projected successor material digest, and derived normalized evaluation digest. Every preparation reconstructs and verifies the proof. Unknown methods, altered sources, copied baseline bindings, modified policy or mismatched digests fail closed. A baseline that already contains a derivation is rejected; corrections always bind the original immutable baseline.

Only the canonical evaluator's proved retained participation fields can enter the derived evaluation: `pool`, `exclusionReason`, `authoritativeAssignmentId` and `mockParticipationEvidence`. The original active/completed qualifications, scoring evidence, rank, seniority, service, specialty, tenure, identity and every other normalized fact remain exact. This restriction matters because the original raw credential capture omitted qualification review holds; a full raw reevaluation is not an authority to restore qualifications withheld in the sealed normalized evaluation.

A changed identity must have one effective authoritative assignment, one approved staffing binding to a reserved position, approved effective staffing, and one reviewed annual assignment term explicitly closed for this bid. Ambiguous holders, missing/unapproved bindings, future or expired assignments, open or unproved terms, and protected leave-participation changes are rejected. No identities or rank exclusions are supplied by the receipt.

The successor mechanically filters only the proved retained identities from the baseline's explicit participant lists and associated frozen provenance. Relative order, stage IDs, kind, comparators, opportunity scope, participant source type and source references are preserved. The original frozen evaluation and raw documents are never rewritten. Creation of a successor version uses the existing semantic Save; existing sessions remain pinned to their original version.

The exact reviewed source packet demonstrates four retained incumbents (two CPT and two LT), producing 218 unique ordinary participants: 20 CPT, 38 LT and 160 FF. The topology remains 231 total positions and 223 open opportunities, including 39 open LT opportunities. These are participation and opportunity counts; they do not indicate that the department is one Lieutenant short.

Pure-helper tests cover legacy binding bytes, copied/tampered receipt fields, source-row digests, altered policy/material, effective-date and approval failures, ambiguous holders, field preservation, and stage projection. The first local runtime regression gate passed 169 cases in seven files, including 46 dedicated derivation and migrated-D1 cases. The D1 tests prepare a successor saved through the normal semantic Save, preserve the historical baseline and freeze, and reject altered receipts without preparation writes. Additional regressions reject recursive derivation and caller-supplied source evaluations or retention IDs at public Mock and Live endpoints. Exact-source offline proof verifies the canonical 218-participant successor and stage compilation. No production source, version or session was written; production Save and acceptance remain separate gates.
