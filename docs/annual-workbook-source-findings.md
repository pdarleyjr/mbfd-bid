This is source analysis for the configurable annual Bid candidate, not approval of a policy or acceptance of a historical replay. Raw workbooks, personnel rows and extracted review files remain outside Git.

## Additional 2025 source verification

An explicitly enabled legacy parser test located the original local `2025 Bid position requirements and points.xlsx` (SHA-256 `9cee1412fca281fbf09c0dde41a71c7618a9410fcc76edc0d956728d789926db`). Its historical test description was incorrect: the first worksheet is a 297-row narrative `Rules & Points` sheet, not a wide qualification matrix. Its separate `Credentials` sheet has the headers `FY25 Cert #`, `FY25 Certifications`, `FY25 Points`, followed by 57 unique credential definitions numbered 1–57. Observed point values are 48 one-point, seven zero-point and two two-point entries. These source values are not an approved current catalog mapping.

The old assertion that the wide-matrix parser should extract at least thirty names from this workbook failed on the actual file. The corrected test pins the source hash and verifies rejection of its incompatible first-sheet format; the parser now reports absent qualification headers explicitly. This is real-source negative import coverage, not a substitute for positive credential extraction or historical eligibility/scoring/outcome replay.

Two other local historical files were located: `MASTER 2025 Bid Positions Selection V18 - FINAL.xlsx` (SHA-256 `3e72d205f83e921593ea3c3a6b33c3810a42ba67c6c206b6f39045133389e038`, nineteen worksheets) and `2025 Bid Policy v3.docx` (SHA-256 `d00437160dcfa11cf9ad754bc87b3f56fb34eb29bbc5952c96794b28dd6c45cb`, 278 paragraphs). The policy contains **83 tracked insertions and 52 tracked deletions**. Its filename does not establish an approved final text; accepting or rejecting those revisions would require authority. Initial whole-node text extraction mixed deleted and retained text and was corrected to read text runs explicitly while preserving the revision counts. No source document was edited. Further historical reconciliation remains outstanding.

Independent XML extraction of the 2025 master found identical populated cell addresses, cached values, cell types, formulas and shared-formula attributes across all nineteen corresponding sheets of the supplied 2026-named master. This is table-content equivalence, not byte identity, formatting equivalence, approval of the applicable period or proof of official completion. The missing caches and seven cached A-Day `#REF!` errors occur in both.

Exact-identifier reconciliation of `Bid Pick` found 231 populated selections, consecutively numbered 1–231, with no duplicate pick numbers, employees or selected positions. All 231 employee and position identifiers resolve in the same workbook's 238-member `Personnel` and 234-position `Positions` tables. All 231 selected employee identifiers also occur among the historical credential PDF's 235 row leaders; all 235 PDF identifiers occur in the master personnel table. No names were used for linkage. Seven master members and three master positions lack a pick; three master members lack a PDF row leader. These counts describe source coverage, not authorization to exclude members, declare vacancies, or assert eligibility, scores, priority, amendments or accepted awards. Private row-level evidence is retained outside Git.

The replay harness now requires explicit rule versions when multiple versions exist for a position and links amendment evidence to independently asserted before/after cases for the same member and position. It reports observed differences separately from approved-expectation failures, while either still fails a claimed exact replay. An enabled historical suite requires approved negative cases, ordering and amendment coverage. Synthetic harness tests pass; no approved historical manifest or meaningful historical replay is claimed.

## Policy document comparison

Both policy DOCX files contain 239 paragraphs with identical extracted body text and no tracked insertions/deletions. A read-only OOXML comparison also found identical direct run-format properties for the corresponding paragraphs. Their XML is not otherwise identical: document properties, paragraph properties, styles, numbering, footnotes, footer 2 and settings differ. The local copy identifies revision 3 modified August 25, 2026; the archive identifies revision 2 modified August 18, 2026. Those metadata values do not establish policy approval.

Both documents were opened read-only in installed Word and exported to private PDFs with macros disabled and link updates disabled for the operation. Their original SHA-256 values remained unchanged. Each export has nine pages. All nine page pairs have identical extracted text, page dimensions and rendered pixels at 100 DPI. All nine distinct page images were visually inspected. The supplied formatting is visibly crowded against the header on pages 3, 5 and 9; this occurs in both copies and has not been repaired in the original documents. Their visible headers say July 2026. The rendered copies are visually equivalent under this renderer; different metadata is not evidence of a different policy body or approval.

The rendered footnotes on pages 1–2 clarify the three-consecutive-cycle condition for annual reopening of Days assignments. Page 6, Marine Operations Training 8(f)(i), states that the training period runs from the **start of the bid**, not from the person's final award. The typed post-award capability therefore supports a separately reviewed, explicit approved bid start date as well as final-award timing. The editor never substitutes session creation, bidding day or assignment start for that approved date. No date or policy interpretation was activated during this analysis.

The 57-page historical credential PDF was structurally reviewed across every page. Its metadata identifies `2025 Annual Bid Calculations Version v8.xlsx` and generation on May 1, 2025. Both pypdf and Poppler's layout extraction found the same 235 unique employee row leaders within a straight-seniority range of 1–238, with no duplicate row leaders. Visual inspection of pages 3–4, 22 and 36–37 confirmed that the printed source itself skips straight-seniority 17, 82 and 133; these are not extractor omissions. Records continue across those page boundaries. The reason for the source gaps is not established, and no absent employee or credential record has been invented. This count is not a canonical roster completeness claim. Applicable historical qualification dates and reviewed policy mappings remain necessary before using a normative replay fixture.
