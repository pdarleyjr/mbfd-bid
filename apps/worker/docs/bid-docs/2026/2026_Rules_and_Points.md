# MBFD 2026 Bid — Rules & Points

> **Derived from**: `2025 Bid position requirements and points.xlsx` → `Rules & Points`
> **Key changes for 2026**:
> 1. All rule blocks titled **"Station 5 - …"** are renamed and apply to **Station 6**.
> 2. The **Station 4 - DE Position** and **Station 4 - All Other Positions** rule
>    blocks now apply to the generic "Stations 1, 3, & 4" group. Station 4 has
>    **no marine cert requirements** in 2026.
> 3. The **Float Captain** rule (which previously carried marine reqs because
>    Float Captain lived at Station 2 / Foam 2 but covered marine ops) keeps
>    its existing SO+MO point tie-break — it is the only cross-division roving
>    captain seat and still needs to be marine-capable.
> 4. Air Tech, Investigator, Captain 5, Station 2 Special Ops blocks: **unchanged**.

## 8. Operations / Technician Cert Inventory (gates points)

Six paired certifications. **Holding all 6 Operations is a prerequisite to score
any Technician points** in any rule block with "Operations gates Technician".

| Operations Cert | Paired Technician Cert |
|-----------------|------------------------|
| Hazardous Materials Operations | State Certified Hazardous Materials Technician |
| Rope Rescue Operations | Rope Rescue Technician |
| Confined Space Operations | Confined Space Technician |
| Structural Collapse Operations | Structural Collapse Technician |
| Trench Rescue Operations | Trench Rescue Technician |
| Vehicle & Machinery Rescue Operations | Vehicle & Machinery Rescue Technician |

## 9. Point-Pool Definitions for Tie-Breaks

- **SO (Special Operations)** = six Operations + six Technician + Drone (+ any block-specific items like AT-cert, DE-cert, Car Seat).
- **MO (Marine Operations)** = MMC + IADRS + Open Water Diver + PSD + Hazmat Ops/Awareness + Car Seat.
- **SO + MO** = arithmetic sum of the two pools (used only by the Float Captain rule).

Always evaluated AFTER total Points Preference is compared and tied.
