## Station #6 — Marine (NEW for 2026)

| Slot | Unit | Rank/Position | Track | Marine reqs |
|------|------|---------------|-------|-------------|
| XX611 | Fire Boat | **Firefighter FBO** *(Fire Boat Operator)* | Combat | **Yes — all** |
| XX612 | Fire Boat | **Marine Firefighter** | Combat | **Yes — all** |
| XX613 | Fire Boat | **Post St.6** | Combat | **Yes — all** |

> Numbering note: the legacy 2025 template used `XX411 / XX412 / XX413` (Float 4)
> for these crew positions. For 2026 they are renumbered `XX611 / XX612 / XX613`
> to reflect their new station home and to avoid confusion in audit logs.

**Eligibility** (all three positions): see `2026_Rules_and_Points.md` — minimum is
Firefighter rank + IADRS Watermanship + Hazmat Awareness + Open Water Diver + MMC.
**Tie-break**: Marine Operations (MO) points → Rank Seniority.

The Fire Boat Operator (XX611) additionally requires **Driver Engineer**
qualification (1 pt Fire Apparatus Op + 1 pt FFP1301 Hydraulics, or 2 pts FL
Pump Operator). The Post St.6 position is the "Floating Firefighter" for the
marine division and carries the same minimum as the Deckhand role.
