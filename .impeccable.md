# MBFD Bid Design Context

## Users

- Command Staff prepare and publish the annual executable Bid policy.
- Authorized Bid operators run the live event under time pressure.
- Authenticated department members follow progress through a read-only presentation surface.

## Brand and tone

- Authoritative, calm, operational, and public-service oriented.
- Prefer direct labels and visible state over decorative language.
- Never visually imply that unconfigured policy is approved or ready.

## Visual direction

- Refined fire-department operations console: dark slate administrative surfaces, restrained MBFD red accents, and high-contrast light presentation surfaces.
- Use the existing Plus Jakarta Sans and Source Sans 3 typography and repository design tokens.
- Dense configuration screens must remain scannable through clear grouping, stable alignment, and concise validation.
- The department presentation should be legible at room distance and must omit administrative or sensitive evidence.

## Interaction principles

1. Policy truth is always visible: real source members and positions, explicit permissions, and blocking unconfigured semantics.
2. Execution controls and audience presentation controls are distinct.
3. Operator workflows optimize for fast, deterministic decisions without moving authority into the browser.
4. Interfaces are mobile-first, keyboard accessible, WCAG AA, touch-friendly, and respectful of reduced-motion preferences.
5. Empty, loading, error, held, paused, and complete states are explicit and cannot be mistaken for live readiness.
